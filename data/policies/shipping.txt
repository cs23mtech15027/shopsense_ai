Shipping and Delivery

Order status is available from the order details page or customer support. An order
marked shipped is in transit, but this status alone does not provide a delivery date.
Delivery estimates and carrier scans must be checked against the current tracking
record. Do not infer a carrier, tracking number, or delivery estimate if one is not
provided in the order record.

If an order appears delayed, customers may contact support with their order number.
Support should verify the current order record before describing delivery progress.
