Peak Sale Delivery Delays

During a peak sale, order processing and carrier scans may take longer than usual.
Customers should be given only the latest estimate shown in the verified order
record. A peak-sale delay does not create a guaranteed delivery date, automatic
shipping compensation, or an exception to the return policy.

If an order is delayed, verify its current status and estimated delivery date before
replying. If the estimate has passed or tracking has not updated, apologize and
escalate the delivery inquiry for a fulfillment review. Do not invent a carrier,
tracking event, delivery date, or compensation.
