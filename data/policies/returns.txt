Returns and Refunds

Eligible items may be returned within 30 calendar days after delivery. An item must
be unused, in its original condition, and include the original tags and proof of
purchase. Final-sale items, personalized items, and items marked non-returnable at
purchase are not eligible for return unless required by applicable law.

After a returned item is received and inspected, an approved refund is issued to the
original payment method. Processing time depends on the payment provider. Submitting
a return request does not mean a refund has been approved or issued.

To request a return, provide the order number to customer support. Refunds require
approval and must not be promised before the return has been reviewed.
