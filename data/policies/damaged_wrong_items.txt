Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Items

When a customer reports damage, a defect, or receiving the wrong product, apologize
and collect the order number, affected product, and a concise description of the
issue. Request photographs only through an approved secure support channel. Do not
ask a customer to post payment details, identity documents, or sensitive personal
information in chat.

Verify the order and compare its product and fulfilment details with the customer
report. A complaint alone does not establish return eligibility, fault, or refund
approval. Route the case for the retailer's documented review. Do not promise a
replacement, refund, shipping reimbursement, or exception unless the relevant
policy and authorized decision confirm it.

If the customer reports an immediate safety hazard, advise them to stop using the
product and escalate to a human support representative promptly. Record the claim
factually without diagnosing injury or assigning fault.
