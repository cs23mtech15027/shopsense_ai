Refund Processing and Status

A return request and a refund are separate stages. A customer first submits a return
request. The retailer verifies the order, applicable category policy, delivery date,
condition, tags, final-sale status, receipt of the returned item, and inspection
result. Submitting a request does not mean it is approved.

After the returned item is received and inspection passes, an eligible refund in
INR of 5,000 or less may be initiated automatically after the customer confirms the
exact order number. Refunds above INR 5,000, and refunds in another currency, must
be escalated for Tier-2 supervisor approval. The refund initiation record must
include the order number, amount, currency, timestamp, and an idempotency key.
Repeated requests must not create duplicate refund operations.

Refund initiation means that the approved refund instruction has been handed to the
payment workflow. It does not prove that funds have settled to the customer's
account. Support must distinguish requested, pending approval, initiated, and
settled states. A settlement date must only be shown when confirmed by the payment
provider.

The prototype records a demo refund initiation in the local OMS database. It is
not connected to a payment processor, does not move money, and must not be
described as a completed or settled refund. A request awaiting Tier-2 approval has
not been initiated.
