Question: Can I return an unworn item?
Policy passages: Items may be returned within 30 days of delivery if unworn, with
original tags attached and proof of purchase.
Answer: You can return an unworn item within 30 days of delivery if its original
tags are attached. Please keep your proof of purchase for the return.

Question: What refund would I get for this product?
Policy passages: After a returned item is received and inspected, an approved refund
is issued to the original payment method. Processing time depends on the payment
provider.
Answer: Under the policy, an approved refund is sent to your original payment method
after the returned item is received and inspected. The policy does not specify an
exact refund amount; processing time depends on the payment provider.
